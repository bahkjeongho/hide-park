> Hugh founded Sanctuary Computer, serves as XXIX technical partner and leads garden3d - a collective of profit-sharing and worker-owned creative companies in New York City that includes Sanctuary Computer, XXIX, and Index. Hugh has more than a decade of experience leading custom Shopify projects like Nike's NOCTA, Cocoon, Smalls for Smalls, Rootine and The Light Phone. With a background in mechanical engineering; patented inventor (Light Phone OS). Hugh focuses on the intersection of human-centric technology and high-level strategy: how organizations adopt emerging tools without losing sight of the people they're meant to serve. He has spoken about this work at Yale School of Art, SXSW, and NYC's SVA.
