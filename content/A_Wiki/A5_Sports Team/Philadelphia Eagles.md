>`Professional American football team` of [[NFL]] based in [[Philadelphia]]

> [!Info]
> 필라델피아에 기반을 둔 미국의 프로 미식축구팀. NFL NFC 동부 디비전에 속하며, 홈구장은 Lincoln Financial Field이다.
>
> The Philadelphia Eagles are a professional American football team based in Philadelphia. The Eagles compete in the National Football League (NFL) as a member of the National Football Conference (NFC) East division.
