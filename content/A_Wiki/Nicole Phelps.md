> [!Info]
> Nicole Phelps is the Global Director of Vogue Runway and Vogue Business, a role she has held since fall 2025. Previously, she served as Director of Vogue Runway from 2015, overseeing all fashion-show coverage on [Vogue.com](http://vogue.com/) and working with staff and freelance contributors to review thousands of runway shows each year.  
  
After graduating from Wesleyan University, Phelps moved to New York City and, following a brief stint in public relations, began her career in fashion journalism at WWD and W magazine. She later spent five years as Fashion News Editor at Elle before joining [Style.com](http://style.com/), where she served for a decade as Executive Editor, reviewing runway collections in New York, Milan, and Paris while helping shape the influential site’s daily editorial coverage.  
  
Phelps wrote the introduction to Maria Cornejo: Zero, published by Rizzoli in September 2026. She lives in Manhattan’s East Village with her husband and son.
