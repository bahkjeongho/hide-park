> Part Brazilian, part Dutch and also a musician, Marcelo loves to create rhythm in images.
> My name is Marcelo Vogelaar and I am a film editor, with a degree in Audiovisual Studies from Centro Universitário Senac in São Paulo.

Over the past 15 years, I have worked across advertising, music videos, short films, documentaries, television programs, and streaming series, consistently focusing on shaping rhythm, structure, and narrative through editing.

I have collaborated with internationally recognized clients and platforms including Netflix, Paramount+, Google, Amazon, Nike, Samsung, KLM, Verizon, Pinterest, Lexus, and Budweiser. My work includes international productions carried out in the United States, England, Germany, the Netherlands, South Korea, Denmark, and Mexico.

I have edited music videos for artists such as Selena Gomez, DJ Snake, Tove Lo, Anderson .Paak, Anitta, Pabllo Vittar, Duda Beat, Criolo, Marina Sena, and Laurent Voulzy.

I currently work as a freelance editor based in São Paulo, collaborating on international productions both remotely and on-site. In the United States and Canada, I am professionally represented by Church Edit (www.edit.church), acting as my agent for editorial engagements and productions.

I speak Portuguese, English, and Spanish.
