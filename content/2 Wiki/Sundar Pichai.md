> [!Info]
> Pichai Sundararajan(1972년 6월 10일생), 흔히 Sundar Pichai는 2015년부터 Google의 CEO, 2019년부터 모회사 Alphabet Inc.의 CEO를 맡고 있는 인도계 미국인 경영인이다. 재료 엔지니어로 경력을 시작했고 컨설팅 회사 McKinsey & Co.를 잠시 거쳐 2004년 Google에 입사해 Google Chrome과 ChromeOS 등 클라이언트 소프트웨어 제품군의 제품 관리와 혁신을 이끌었고, Google 드라이브 개발을 주도했다. Gmail과 Google 지도 같은 다른 앱의 개발도 총괄했다.
>
> Pichai Sundararajan (born June 10, 1972), better known as Sundar Pichai, is an Indian–American business executive who has been the CEO of Google since 2015 and the CEO of its parent company Alphabet Inc. since 2019.
>
> Pichai began his career as a materials engineer. Following a short stint at the management consulting firm McKinsey & Co., Pichai joined Google in 2004, where he led the product management and innovation efforts for a suite of Google's client software products, including Google Chrome and ChromeOS, as well as being largely responsible for Google Drive. In addition, he went on to oversee the development of other applications such as Gmail and Google Maps.
