> [!Info]
> 뉴올리언스에 기반을 둔 미국의 프로 미식축구팀. NFL NFC 남부 디비전에 속하며, 홈구장은 Caesars Superdome이다.

> The New Orleans Saints are a professional American football team based in New Orleans. The Saints compete in the National Football League (NFL) as a member of the National Football Conference (NFC) South division.
