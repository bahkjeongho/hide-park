>`Professional American football team` of [[NFL]] based in [[East Rutherford]]

> [!Info]
> 이스트러더퍼드에 기반을 둔 미국의 프로 미식축구팀. NFL AFC 동부 디비전에 속하며, 홈구장은 MetLife Stadium이다.
>
> The New York Jets are a professional American football team based in the New York metropolitan area. The Jets compete in the National Football League (NFL) as a member of the American Football Conference (AFC) East division.
