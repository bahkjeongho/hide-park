>
> 1965년 이래 우리 프로그램은 문화·예술 지형에 큰 영향을 끼쳐 왔다.
>
> Our program mentors young artists in the traditions and innovations of an evolving media landscape that is deeply rooted in visual storytelling. Together, our faculty, staff, and students create more than 7,000 films per year.
>
> Since 1965, our program has had a significant impact on the cultural and artistic landscape.
