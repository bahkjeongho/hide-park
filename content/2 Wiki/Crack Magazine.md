> [!Info]
> Crack Magazine is a British music channel which started as a DIY newspaper for music obsessives and grew into a definitive, global media brand on a mission to make people care about music. Still publishing every month, still independent and always free. No algorithms, no parent companies, no bad music. From publishing star-making debut cover stories with the likes of Dave, FKA twigs, Central Cee and Beabadoobee to historic profiles on generational innovators like Charli xcx, Kylie, Aphex Twin and PJ Harvey – the magazine’s critical reputation is built on careful curation and thoughtful storytelling where music is brought to life through words and visuals. Follow @crackmagazine and you’ll be plugged into a stream of cultural commentary, insightful explainers and eye-opening stories from music and beyond. Having expanded into an award-winning creative agency (CC Co.) and a much-loved festival (Simple Things), Crack Magazine continues to set the standard for work made by, and for, fans who believe in the power of great music.
