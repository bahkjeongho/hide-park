>
> Graphic design is everywhere. It’s powering the apps on your phone, driving the opening titles of your favorite TV series and surrounding you at stadium concerts. While pursuing a Bachelor of Fine Arts in Graphic Design from ArtCenter, you’ll learn how to turn your ideas into powerful visuals that inspire, influence and get noticed. The College’s Graphic Design program offers a rigorous, hands-on curriculum that emphasizes depth, experimentation and real-world experiences in an environment that embraces the latest technological advances.
