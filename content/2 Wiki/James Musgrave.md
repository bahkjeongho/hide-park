> James Musgrave is the Creative Director of XXIX with 18 years of experience. At XXIX he's lead projects for clients including Google, Hinge, Brooklyn Museum and Magic Leap. He's also lead our engagement with Salt & Stone for the last four years working across brand, web and packaging contributing 1000x growth in revenue. Before joining XXIX, he worked in London for clients including Alexander McQueen, Instagram, Louis Vuitton, Sir Paul McCartney, Penguin Books, Phaidon, Warp Records, Universal Music, V&A, Hayward Gallery and the Serpentine Gallery. He is a four-time Grammy nominee, one-time winner, and former judge for the Recording Academy. He's also a D&AD award winner and new blood mentor. He's spoken about design at conferences including Google Span and It's Nice That x Adobe.
