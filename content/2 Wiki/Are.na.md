> [!Info]
> Are.na is a place to save content, create collections over time and connect ideas. Privately or with other people.

Students (highly curious and open to new information), hobbyists (deeply into a topic or topics, narrowly focused) or what we call connected knowledge collectors (those more experienced but highly curious information gatherers who can make disparate connections between disciplines) have been the core of our community for **15 years and 72 days**.

Are.na is a mindful space where you can work through any project over time. It's a place to structure your ideas and build new forms of knowledge together.

> [!Summary]
