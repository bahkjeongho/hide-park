>`Master's Program` in Photography of [[RCA]]

> [!Info]
> 왕립예술대학 사진 MA 프로그램은 사진을 실천의 중심에 두고 예술가로 성장할 수 있는 환경을 제공합니다. 이 프로그램은 동시대 사진 실천과 이미지 제작의 최전선에서 일하는 예술가와 작가들로 이루어진 독특한 학제간 공동체입니다. 우리는 사진을 정지·동영상, 출판, 퍼포먼스, 설치, 사회적 실천, 사운드, 합성 미디어, 텍스트, 글쓰기를 포괄하는 확장하고 진화하는 실천으로 이해합니다.
>
> The Photography MA programme at the Royal College of Art provides an environment where you can develop as an artist with photography at the core of your practice. The MA Photography programme at the RCA comprises a unique, cross-disciplinary community of artists and writers working at the cutting edge of contemporary photographic practice and image-making. We understand photography to be an expansive and evolving practice, which includes the still and moving image, publishing, performance, installation, social practice, sound, synthetic media, text, and writing. Central to our approach is the practising, theorising, and receiving of photography as a field unrestricted by the conventions of material, medium, format, discipline, and genre. Important to our programme ethos is a commitment to process; to the unknown, indeterminate, speculative, and hybrid.
