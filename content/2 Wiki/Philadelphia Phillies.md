> [!Info]
> 필라델피아에 기반을 둔 미국의 프로 야구팀. MLB 내셔널 리그 동부 디비전에 속하며, 홈구장은 Citizens Bank Park이다.
>
> The Philadelphia Phillies are an American professional baseball team based in Philadelphia. The Phillies compete in Major League Baseball (MLB) as a member club of the National League (NL) East Division.
