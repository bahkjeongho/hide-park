>`Bachelor` in architectural design of [[Gerrit Rietveld Academie]]

> [!Info]
> Architectural Design 학과는 성찰, 제작, 사변을 통해 건축 환경의 생산에 참여하는 데 집중합니다. 공간 지능과 건축적 사고를 결합한 공간 실천의 발전을 북돋우며, 학생들은 사변적 디자인 스튜디오, 현실 협업, 이론 세미나, 리서치 랩을 통해 사건·사물·시스템·경험·장소의 관계를 생각하고 개입하는 법을 배웁니다.
>
> The Architectural Design department focuses on engaging with the production of the built environment through reflection, fabrication, and speculation. Students work at the crossroads of the real and the imagined; the past, the present, and the future. Through speculative design studios, real-world collaborations, theory seminars, and research labs, they learn to think about and intervene within the relations between events, things, systems, experiences, and places.
