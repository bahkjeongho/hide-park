>`Professional American football team` of [[NFL]] based in [[Denver]]

> [!Info]
> 덴버에 기반을 둔 미국의 프로 미식축구팀. NFL AFC 서부 디비전에 속하며, 홈구장은 Empower Field at Mile High이다.

> The Denver Broncos are a professional American football team based in Denver. The Broncos compete in the National Football League (NFL) as a member of the American Football Conference (AFC) West division.
