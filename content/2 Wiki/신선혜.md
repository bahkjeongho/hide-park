> Shin SunHye is a photographer based in Seoul. Shin studied photography in Korea and Italy before launching her own career.  
  
Her ability to capture the moment is often expressed through a love of light, she also expertly deploys colour. Influenced by her love of nature and light, Shin's beautifully lit images capture a sense of beauty that portrays person in a graceful and engaging way.  
  
Her curiosity and personality has brought her to create a wide number of projects : Lookbooks, print advertisings and editorials.
