> [!Info]
> Hong Janghyun was raised in Korea and fled to US to persue photography before completing his B.A in 2003. Through the years he made his own way into photography, now he has a highly influential role in fashion industry.  
  
He is skilled with international experience in complex photoshoots for worldwide magazines, with experiences of assignments in the US, Europe and Asia. He is always looking for true images and shows a never ending interest in realism and timeless qualities. He has been featured in magazines and his work has been exhibited throughout galleries in Korea.
