>Facebook과 Meta Platforms를 공동 창업한 미국 사업가·프로그래머

> [!Info]
> Mark Elliot Zuckerberg(1984년 5월 14일생)는 소셜 미디어 서비스 Facebook과 그 모회사 Meta Platforms를 공동 창업한 미국의 사업가이자 웹마스터, 프로그래머다. 현재 Meta의 이사회 의장, CEO이자 지배 주주다. 하버드 대학교를 잠시 다녔고, 2004년 2월 룸메이트 Eduardo Saverin, Andrew McCollum, Dustin Moskovitz, Chris Hughes와 함께 Facebook을 출범시켰으며, 2012년 5월 회사를 상장했다. Chan Zuckerberg Initiative 설립을 포함해 여러 대규모 기부를 해 왔다. 그의 초기 경력과 Facebook의 성공을 다룬 영화 <소셜 네트워크>가 2010년 개봉했다.
>
> Mark Elliot Zuckerberg (; born May 14, 1984) is an American businessman, webmaster, and programmer who co-founded the social media service Facebook and its parent company Meta Platforms. He is its chairman, chief executive officer (CEO), and controlling shareholder.
>
> Zuckerberg briefly attended Harvard College, and launched Facebook there in February 2004 with his roommates Eduardo Saverin, Andrew McCollum, Dustin Moskovitz and Chris Hughes. Zuckerberg took the company public in May 2012 with majority shares.
>
> He has used his funds to organize multiple large donations, including the establishment of the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative. A film based on Zuckerberg's early career, legal troubles and initial success with Facebook, The Social Network, was released in 2010. His prominence and fast rise in the technology industry has prompted political and legal attention. He has been the subject of multiple lawsuits regarding the creation and ownership of the website as well as issues such as user privacy.
