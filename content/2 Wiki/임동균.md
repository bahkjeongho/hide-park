> [!Info]
> Dongkyun Lim is an industrial designer with experience in designing various living products,  
such as furniture, tableware and electronics.  
He graduated from Hongik University and began his career at Motorola. He later pursued an MA in Product Design at ECAL in Switzerland.  
Afterward, he worked as a senior designer at Cecilie Manz Studio for 6years in Copenhagen,Denmark.
