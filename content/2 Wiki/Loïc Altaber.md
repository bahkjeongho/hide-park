> Loïc Altaber is a graphic designer and art director based in Paris, working in the field of culture such as art, cinema, architecture, music and fashion, he creates books, posters (animated or printed), motion design, visual identities and websites. He has developed a typographic approach combined with animation work and has collaborated with graphic design studios Building Paris, Lookspecific and Helmo.
