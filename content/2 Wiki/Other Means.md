> Other Means is a graphic design studio in Brooklyn, NY, interested in finding the productive tensions that emerge from placing one thing next to an unlikely other: historical precedent and contemporary culture, institutional authority and popular vernacular, rigorous systems and their inevitable exceptions.

The studio designs identities and books, builds websites and exhibitions, develops brand strategies and signage systems, and frequently teaches, hosts lectures, and leads workshops. All of these activities are equal to each other and happen at the same time.

Since 2012, Other Means has launched new cultural institutions, reintroduced legacy organizations, and worked one-on-one with directors, artists, and in-house teams. Recent clients include the Yale School of Art, The Guston Foundation, the Medina Triennial, James Fuentes Gallery, Poetry Society of America, and the Brooklyn Museum. In 2025, the studio launched OM Books, a publishing imprint that explores the visual language of the city.

### Studio

Other Means is led by Gary Fogelson, Phil Lubliner, and Ryan Waller. Current staff includes designer Bella De Angelis and developer Remy Hunter. The studio collaborates with type designers, animators, strategists, producers, photographers, writers, and developers to meet the needs of a given project.
