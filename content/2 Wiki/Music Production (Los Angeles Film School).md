> L.A. 필름 스쿨의 한 부문인 로스앤젤레스 레코딩 스쿨은 음악 프로덕션 전문학사(Associate of Science) 학위를 제공합니다. 이 실습 중심 프로그램은 기술 역량을 빠르게 다지고 음악 프로덕션과 아티스트 매니지먼트를 더 폭넓게 이해하도록 도와줍니다.
>
> Make your music stand out with a degree in Music Production. Whether you’re interested in recording, mixing and mastering or something in between, our program teaches you the technical skills needed to enhance your beats and produce top quality tracks.
>
> The Los Angeles Recording School, a division of L.A. Film School offers an Associate of Science Degree in Music Production. This hands-on program will help you sharpen your technical skills quickly and gain a broader understanding of music production and artist management.
