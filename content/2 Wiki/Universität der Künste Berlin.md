> The Universität der Künste Berlin (UdK; also known in English as the Berlin University of the Arts), situated in Berlin, Germany, is the second largest art school in Europe. It is a public art and design school, and one of the four research universities in the city. It has four colleges specialising in fine arts, architecture, media and design, music and the performing arts with around 3,500 students.
