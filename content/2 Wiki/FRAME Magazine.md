>1997년 암스테르담에서 창간한 인테리어·공간 디자인 매거진

> [!Info]
> FRAME은 인테리어 디자인 전문의 첫 번째이자 대표적인 글로벌 출판사다. 대표 매거진, 책, 어워드 프로그램, 디지털 콘텐츠를 통해 디자이너와 업계 전문가에게 앞을 내다보는 영감과 통찰을 제공하여, 공간을 바꾸고 전 세계 사람들의 삶을 개선하도록 힘을 싣는다.
>
> 디자이너, 디자인 전문가, 디자인 지향 브랜드라면 FRAME에 함께하세요. 이 페이지에서 우리가 누구이고 무엇을 제공하는지 알아보세요.

## Member

[**Floor Kuitert (Editor in Chief)**](https://frameweb.com/tags/author/Floor%20Kuitert): Floor is a seasoned writer and editor with over a decade of content strategy experience in interior architecture and design. At FRAME, she leads the editorial team, drawing on her background in creative studios and foresight agencies to shape future-facing content across all publications and events.

[**Britt Berden (Editor)**](https://frameweb.com/tags/author/Britt%20Berden)**:** Britt is a researcher, creative strategist, and (visual) editor with a background in futures thinking. At FRAME, she explores global and local design trends, focusing on visual narratives that bring to life innovation across design, spaces, materials, textiles, and technology.

[**Chieri Higa (Editor)**](https://frameweb.com/tags/author/Chieri%20Higa)**:** Since entering design writing in 2018, Chieri brings a design-educated maker’s eye to her work at FRAME, complemented by a conceptual perspective shaped by her academic background in applied ethics.

[**Hayley Daen (Managing Editor, Digital)**](https://frameweb.com/tags/author/Hayley%20Daen)**:** With years of journalistic and copywriting experience, Hayley Dean brings a sharp editorial eye to FRAME. She works alongside FRAME's content team to create and collate compelling stories and give a platform to seminal voices in the world of design.

Meet the team that brings FRAME’s creative publications to life and maintains the ties with our community.

**Mira Huussen (Managing Director):** Mira combines her studies in Architecture with leadership experience across public and private sectors. She is driven by a commitment to positive impact, progress and collaboration, guiding FRAME’s mission to empower creative communities worldwide.

**Barbara Iwanicka (Design Director):** Barbara is a Polish art director and graphic designer. Since 2012, she has been Design Director at FRAME, leading the development of visual concepts and products across print, branding, digital, and events.

**Devita Pawennei (Creative Services Manager):** Devita joined the FRAME Awards team early on, helping shape the original format that she now leads as Head of FRAME Awards. She also oversees creative projects at FRAME, delivering initiatives and experiences that facilitate networking, knowledge-sharing, and inspiration.

**Renata Sutton (Marketing & Communications Specialist):** Since joining FRAME in 2019, Renata has led integrated campaigns and aligning messaging across channels to strengthen FRAME’s brand presence. She delivers campaigns that engage global design audiences, fostering meaningful connections between FRAME, its partners, and the wider design community.

**Melika Aghabeigi (Account Manager Southern Europe):** Melika supports FRAME’s clients across Southern Europe, leveraging her background in architecture, business design, and international experience. As an Account Manager, she combines industry insight with a passion for building lasting relationships within the design community.
