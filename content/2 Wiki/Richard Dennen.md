> [!Info]
> Richard Dennen was made Editor of Tatler in February 2018. Under Richard's editorship, Tatler now has one of the fastest growing digital audiences of any Condé Nast brand globally, as well as having overtaken both Elle and Harper's Bazaar in UK print subscriptions.

Richard was already intimately acquainted with Tatler having worked for the magazine for 6 years. First with Fashion Director Isabella Blow, before rising through the ranks to the position of Editor-at-Large.  Following that, he worked on Fleet Street at the Sunday Times, The Mail on Sunday and the Evening Standard.
