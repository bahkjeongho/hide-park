> [!Info]
> Melanie McElduff is a graphic designer and project manager with over 20 years of experience developing and managing brands for a varied and sophisticated clientele.

As a designer, brand manager, and creative director, she has worked with institutions and companies including Cornell University, Middlebury College, Princeton University Press, Hearst Corporation, Library of Congress, The Garrison Institute, Satori Software, Vassar College, Lodgeworks, Hirshhorn Museum & Sculpture Garden, Archer Hotels, Presto Packaging, Charlie Palmer Restaurant Group, and the Brennan Center for Justice at NYU, on tasks ranging from identity design and guidelines development, to the design of exhibitions, packaging, and print and digital communications.

She is a three-time recipient of the Sappi, Ideas that Matter design grant. Melanie joined this firm in 2003 after graduating with a BFA in Graphic Design from the University of Illinois.
