> 파리 국립장식미술학교(ÉnsAD, 'Arts Déco')는 PSL 연구대학의 구성원인 공립 그랑제콜 예술·디자인 학교로, 파리 위름 가에 있습니다.
>
> The École nationale supérieure des Arts Décoratifs (ÉnsAD) also known as Arts Decos' and École des Arts décoratifs, is a public grande école of art and design, constituent member of PSL Research University. The school is located in the Rue d'Ulm in Paris.
