>`Master's Program` in Photography of [[SAIC]]

> [!Info]
> 국내 최고 수준으로 평가받는 스튜디오 사진 MFA(Master of Fine Arts in Studio, Photography) 프로그램은 전통적이고 실험적인 이미지 제작 방식을 통해 사회·시각 문화를 끊임없이 재해석합니다. SAIC의 사진 대학원 프로그램은 매체의 잠재력을 탐구하는 데 전념하며, 기술적 고려를 훨씬 넘어서는 교육을 제공합니다. 교수진은 각자의 실천이 매우 다양하며 학생의 다채로운 접근과 탐구를 지원합니다.
>
> The Master of Fine Arts (MFA) in Studio, Photography program, widely considered one of the strongest in the country, is continually reinterpreting social and visual culture through both traditional and experimental forms of image making. SAIC's graduate Photography program is committed to investigating the potential of the medium, providing education that extends far beyond technical considerations. The faculty, extremely diverse in their own practices, support a multiplicity of student approaches and investigations. They train students to be sensitive, perceptive problem-solvers capable of operating as artists, intellectuals, and professionals in complex cultural environments.
