>`Design Week` held annually in [[Eindhoven]]

> [!Info]
> 창의성, 놀라운 아이디어, 영감이 가득한 9일. 네덜란드 디자인 재단은 25년 넘게 Dutch Design Week, Dutch Design Awards, 연합체와 프로그램을 통해 약 1만 명의 디자이너, 약 23만 명의 방문객, 기업과 정책 분야의 혁신가가 모이는 독특한 네트워크를 만들어 왔습니다.
>
> Nine-days bursting with creativity, mind-blowing ideas, and inspiration. For more than 25 years Dutch Design Foundation has brought together a unique network of about 10,000 designers, approximately 230,000 visitors and innovators in business and policy through events such as Dutch Design Week, Dutch Design Awards, coalitions and programmes like Un/School.
