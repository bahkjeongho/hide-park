> [!Info]
> Mark Fisher was an English academic and writer from England, born in July 1968. He worked as a professor at Goldsmiths, University of London. He always had a politically focused mind and in the early 2000’s fell distraught at the worlds state of affairs and the importance of huge corporations and huge stars. Fisher, himself was far more attracted to the obscure when it came to film, music etc. This impassioned him to create a blog in 2003 called K-Punk, which is what he has become most known for. This blog chronicled the start of the digital revolution in the modern world and included commentary on what he viewed as the positive possible outcomes of the internet but also the negativity of what was as the internet became more and more to do with large corporations just as everything else.
