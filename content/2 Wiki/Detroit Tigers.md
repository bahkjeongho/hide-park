>`Professional baseball team` of [[MLB]] based in [[Detroit]]

> [!Info]
> 디트로이트에 기반을 둔 미국의 프로 야구팀. MLB 아메리칸 리그 AL Central 디비전에 속하며, 홈구장은 Comerica Park이다.
>
> The Detroit Tigers are an American professional baseball team based in Detroit. The Tigers play in Major League Baseball (MLB) as a member club of the American League (AL) Central Division.
