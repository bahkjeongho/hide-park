> 왕립 덴마크 아카데미(Architecture, Design, Conservation)는 건축·디자인·보존 분야에서 스칸디나비아를 대표하는 아카데미입니다. 7개 연구소를 기반으로 교육, 연구, 자문, 혁신을 이끌고, 과학·고등교육·디지털부 산하 고등교육기관으로서 건축·디자인·보존의 세 교육 영역을 7개 학과에 걸쳐 제공합니다.
>
> The Royal Danish Academy is the leading academy in Scandinavia in the fields of architecture, design and conservation. Our activities are rooted in 7 institutes, which generate education, research, consultancy and innovation. The Royal Danish Academy - Architecture, Design, Conservation is a higher education institution under the aegis of the Ministry of Science, Higher Education and Digital Affairs. The institution provides 3 main areas of education: architecture, design and conservation, spread across 7 departments.
