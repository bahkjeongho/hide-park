>
> Where Art Shapes Presence.
>
> HAY TIN is an Artist-led Creative Label founded and directed by Haydonna. Through brands, spaces, objects, and experiences, TIN creates contemporary culture through original works and collaborations. Grounded in aesthetic judgment, of form and color, sensibility and style, we articulate the worlds that brands hold within them.
>
> ​
>
> HAY TIN does not view design as a mere outcome of problem-solving. We begin with aesthetics. Guided by the artist’s discernment of form, color, sensibility, and style, we determine how a brand should exist, and translate that vision into a cohesive and consistent direction.
>
> ​
>
> Across products, spaces, content, and experiences, our work begins with an exploration of material and color, proportion and rhythm, and a distinct visual attitude.
>
> Each project is completed as a style, as a scene of its own, where a brand acquires its unique atmosphere and presence.
>
> ​
>
> Haydonna’s practice moves fluidly between form and graphic, artwork and space, designing brands to be perceived as fully realized worlds. Rather than repeating standardized design languages, we propose new aesthetics and new visual orders
>
> that brands have yet to reach.
>
> ​
>
> Collaborating with HAY TIN is not about following conventional formulas. It is a process of shaping a brand’s next chapter through the artist’s vision, in ways that did not previously exist.
