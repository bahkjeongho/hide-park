> `Publisher and Chairman` of [[The New York Times]]

> [!Info]
> Mr. Sulzberger oversees both newsroom and company operations, and is the principal steward of the independence, ambition and excellence of Times journalism.

With a journalism operation of more than 3,000 people reporting from around the globe, The Times is the most influential and award-winning news organization in the world.

Since he became publisher in 2018, Mr. Sulzberger has significantly increased investment in investigative and international reporting, while also pushing The Times to expand into new digital formats like audio, video, interactive and data journalism. He is an outspoken defender of journalistic independence and the rights of the free press in the United States and abroad. 

As one of the architects of the company’s digital transformation and business strategy, he has helped The Times grow its subscribers to more than 13 million, up from 800,000 in 2014, the year he led a team that produced the Innovation Report. 

Before becoming publisher, Mr. Sulzberger worked as a reporter and editor. He began his career at The Providence Journal and The Oregonian before joining The Times as a metro reporter in 2009. He later served as a national correspondent based in Kansas City, assistant metro editor, associate editor for newsroom strategy and deputy publisher. A graduate of Brown University, he is the sixth member of the Ochs-Sulzberger family to serve The Times as publisher since the newspaper was purchased by Adolph Ochs in 1896.
