> [!Info]
> 패션공과대학(FIT)은 뉴욕주립대학교(SUNY)에 속한 뉴욕시의 공립 대학이자 패션 학교로, 패션 산업과 연결된 예술·경영·디자인·매스커뮤니케이션·기술에 중점을 둡니다. 1944년에 설립되었습니다.
>
> The Fashion Institute of Technology (FIT) is a public college and fashion school under the State University of New York, in New York City. It focuses on art, business, design, mass communication, and technology connected to the fashion industry. It was founded in 1944.
