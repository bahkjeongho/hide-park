> [!Info]
> Marc Beaugé is a cultural storyteller.  
  
Trained as a journalist in Paris, Marc Beaugé began his career writing about politics, culture, and sports before  
specializing in fashion journalism. For more than a decade, he has been a columnist for Le Monde and a contributor to Quotidien, France's most-watched late-night television show. He also served as Editor-in-Chief of the renowned HOLIDAY magazine for seven years. In 2018, he launched the successful magazines L'Étiquette, which he continues to oversee today.  
  
Alongside his editorial work, Marc Beaugé advises brands seeking greater cultural relevance and creative impact. Drawing on his wide-ranging experience, he works across creative direction, brand and product consulting, as well as writing, hosting, and moderating conversations.
