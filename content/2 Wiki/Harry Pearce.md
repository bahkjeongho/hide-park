>[!tldr] tl;dr
>2006년부터 Pentagram 런던 사무소의 파트너인 영국 그래픽 디자이너

> [!Info]
> Harry Pearce는 그래픽 디자이너, 우연주의자(accidentalist), 영원한 낙천주의자, 사진가다.
>
> 캔터베리 예술대학에서 공부했다. 2006년 Pentagram의 파트너로 합류하기 전, 16년간 Lippa Pearce Design을 공동 설립·운영했다.
>
> Pearce는 Liberty, Thames & Hudson, Camden Art Centre, WITNESS, John Lewis Partnership, Waitrose & Partners No.1, 왕립예술원, 아부다비 문화지구, Berry Bros & Rudd, Phaidon Press, Pink Floyd Records, Saks Fifth Avenue, Lloyd’s of London, 셰익스피어 글로브, PEN International, 과학박물관, 유엔 등 다양한 클라이언트를 위해 전 세계에서 아이덴티티, 설치, 포스터, 패키지, 책, 강연을 고안해 왔다. Ai Weiwei와 Anish Kapoor의 왕립예술원 회고전 아이덴티티도 만들었다.
>
> 그의 작업은 뉴욕, 파리, 런던, 토론토, 나폴리에서 전시되었다.
>
> 1993년부터 Peter Gabriel이 설립한 인권 자선단체 WITNESS의 자문위원으로 활동해 왔다. 국제그래픽연맹(AGI)의 헌신적인 회원이기도 하며, 호주, 남아프리카공화국, 뉴질랜드, 미국, 인도, 네덜란드, 코스타리카 등 전 세계 디자인 콘퍼런스에서 강연했다.
>
> Pearce는 《Typographic Conundrums》(2009)와 《Eating with the Eyes》(2015) 두 권의 저자다.
>
> Harry Pearce is a graphic designer, accidentalist, eternal optimist and photographer.
>
> He studied at [Canterbury College of Art.](http://www.uca.ac.uk/) Before joining Pentagram as a partner in 2006, he co-founded and co-ran Lippa Pearce Design for 16 years.
>
> Pearce has worked around the world devising identities, installations, posters, packaging, books and talks for clients as diverse as Liberty, Thames & Hudson, Camden Art Centre, WITNESS, The John Lewis Partnership, Waitrose & Partners No.1, the Royal Academy of Arts, Abu Dhabi cultural quarter, Berry Bros & Rudd, Phaidon Press, Pink Floyd Records, Saks Fifth Avenue, Lloyd’s of London, Shakespeare’s Globe, PEN International, Science Museum and the UN. For Ai Weiwei and Anish Kapoor, he created identities for their major retrospectives at the RA.
>
> His work has been exhibited in New York, Paris, London, Toronto and Naples.
>
> Since 1993 he has been an active member of the advisory board for [WITNESS,](http://witness.org/) a human rights charity founded by Peter Gabriel. He is also a committed member of [Alliance Graphique Internationale](http://a-g-i.org/) and has spoken at design conferences across the globe including Australia, South Africa, New Zealand, USA, India, Holland and Costa Rica.
>
> Pearce is the author of two books, [_Typographic Conundrums_](http://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/0061826596) (published in 2009) and [_Eating with the Eyes_](https://uniteditions.com/) (published in 2015).
