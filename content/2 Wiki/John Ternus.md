> [!Info]
> John Patrick Ternus(1975년 5월생)는 2026년 9월부터 Apple의 최고경영자(CEO)를 맡고 있는 미국의 엔지니어이자 경영인이다. 2001년 Apple 제품 디자인팀에 합류했고, 2021년부터 2026년까지 하드웨어 엔지니어링 수석 부사장을 지냈다. Apple은 그가 iPad와 AirPods 제품군 작업에 기여했다고 평가하며, 맥이 인텔 프로세서에서 애플 실리콘으로 전환하던 시기에 하드웨어 엔지니어링을 총괄했다.
>
> John Patrick Ternus (; born May 1975) is an American engineer and business executive who has been the chief executive officer (CEO) of Apple since September 2026. Ternus joined Apple's product design team in 2001, and was the company's senior vice president of hardware engineering from 2021 to 2026.
>
> Apple has credited Ternus with work on the iPad and AirPods product lines, and he oversaw hardware engineering during the company's transition from Intel processors to Apple silicon in Mac computers. Upon inheriting Dan Riccio's position as senior vice president of hardware engineering, it has been widely reported by tech analysts that he has radically changed Apple's hardware and design philosophy, pivoting Apple's hardware engineering into prioritizing function over form, marking a major departure from Jony Ive's form over function focus for Apple products.
