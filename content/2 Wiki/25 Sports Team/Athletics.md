> [!Info]
> 웨스트새크라멘토에 기반을 둔 미국의 프로 야구팀. MLB 아메리칸 리그 서부 디비전에 속하며, 홈구장은 Sutter Health Park이다.
>
> The Athletics (often referred to as the A's) are an American professional baseball team in Major League Baseball (MLB) and a member club of the American League (AL) West Division. The team plays its home games at Sutter Health Park in West Sacramento, California, and is approved to relocate to the Las Vegas metropolitan area for the 2028 season.
