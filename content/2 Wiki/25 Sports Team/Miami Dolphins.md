>`Professional American football team` of [[NFL]] based in [[Miami Gardens]]

> [!Info]
> 마이애미가든스에 기반을 둔 미국의 프로 미식축구팀. NFL AFC 동부 디비전에 속하며, 홈구장은 Hard Rock Stadium이다.
>
> The Miami Dolphins are a professional American football team based in the Miami metropolitan area. The Dolphins compete in the National Football League (NFL) as a member of the American Football Conference (AFC) East division.
