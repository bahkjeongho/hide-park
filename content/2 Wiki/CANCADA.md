> [!Info]
> CANADA is a creative production company founded in 2008 when a small collective of directors came together, united by the desire to connect with an audience via meaningful, well-crafted films.

Since then, we have grown to become a family of international creatives and producers with offices in Barcelona, London and Los Angeles.

Our shared love for our work, our belief in the power of collaboration, the magic of people, and the wonder of true artistry unite everything we do.

With decades of experience creating award-winning work by our directors and photographers, we’re proud to develop and produce our projects with a global approach.
