> [!Info]
> Founded in 2009, SAY WHO is an international media platform, photography agency, content production studio and image licensing platform specializing in the worlds of fashion, luxury, art, design and culture.

Our mission is to **observe, document and amplify** the people, institutions, brands and events shaping today’s creative industries. Through our editorial platform, we chronicle the cultural landscape by covering the moments that matter: Fashion Weeks, art fairs, biennials, exhibitions, festivals, museum openings, brand launches and private events.

Our ambition goes beyond reporting the news. We are building **a visual archive of our time**—one that captures the people, places and cultural moments defining contemporary creativity.******
