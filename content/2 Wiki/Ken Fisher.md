> `Founder & Editor-in-Chief` of [[ars techinica]]

> [!Info]
> Ken Fisher is the founder and Editor-in-Chief of Ars Technica, a leading destination for IT professionals and technologists. An IT veteran with academic expertise in ancient languages and history, Fisher has transformed Ars Technica from a niche publication for PC enthusiasts into a world-class technology journal. Prior to starting Ars Technica, Fisher worked in IT for 12 years at Indiana University and Harvard University. Simultaneously, he earned two BAs, two master's degrees and an ABD Ph.D. at Harvard University.
