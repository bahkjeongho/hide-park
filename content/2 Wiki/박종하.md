> Korea based photographer Park Jong Ha studied photography in Korea. After graduation, he continues photography as a profession to explore unique identities and places through his camera lens in search of a specific mood in his image.  
  
His photographic foundation is rooted with moody undertones and his images shows a youthful energy. In order to produce rich distinctive images that express a deep narrative, he uses suitable light, location and subject.

  
JongHa's enthusiasm for his work are what make him a standout among his peers and will surely keep him busy for years to come.
