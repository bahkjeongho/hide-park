> [!Info]
> Sergey Mikhailovich Brin(러시아어: Сергей Михайлович Брин, 1973년 8월 21일생)은 Larry Page와 함께 Google을 공동 창업한 미국의 컴퓨터 과학자이자 사업가다. 2019년 물러날 때까지 Google 모회사 Alphabet의 사장이었고, Page와 함께 공동 창업자, 지배 주주, 이사로 Alphabet에 남아 있다. 모스크바의 러시아계 유대인 가정에서 태어나 여섯 살에 미국으로 이민했고, 메릴랜드 대학교 칼리지파크에서 수학과 컴퓨터 과학을 공부했다. 1993년 스탠퍼드 대학교 컴퓨터 과학 박사 과정에 진학해 Page를 만나 웹 검색 엔진을 만들었고, 박사 과정을 그만두고 멘로파크에서 Google을 시작했다.
>
> Sergey Mikhailovich Brin (Russian: Сергей Михайлович Брин; born August 21, 1973) is an American computer scientist and businessman who co-founded Google with Larry Page. He was the president of Google's parent company, Alphabet Inc., until stepping down from the role in 2019. He and Page remain at Alphabet as co-founders, controlling shareholders, and board members.
>
> Born into a Russian-Jewish family in Moscow, Soviet Union, Brin immigrated to the United States at the age of six. He earned his bachelor's degree at the University of Maryland, College Park, following in his father's and grandfather's footsteps by studying mathematics as well as computer science. After graduation, in 1993, he enrolled in Stanford University to pursue a PhD in computer science. There, Brin met Page, with whom he built a web search engine. The program became popular at Stanford, and he discontinued his PhD studies to start Google in Susan Wojcicki's garage in Menlo Park, California. In 2023, Brin came out of retirement to contribute to AI research at Alphabet.
