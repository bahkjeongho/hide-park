> [!Info]
> #### 23 is a London based creative agency founded by Matt Roach + Emma Hetherington. Newest member of the team is Seven, pictured below.  
  
A graduate of the London College of Printing, Matt started off as art director of Dazed & Confused magazine in late nineties Shoreditch, working concurrently on various projects with talents such as Alexander McQueen, John Galliano, Katie Grand, Katy England, Nick Knight and Nick Waplington.  
  
After graduating with first class honours from the Central Saint Martins Fine Art Film + Video degree course, Emma trained with some of the world’s leading cinematographers at Ridley Scott Associates. She went on to specialise in creating 16mm films for various music and fashion clients from Kylie Minogue to Gucci.
