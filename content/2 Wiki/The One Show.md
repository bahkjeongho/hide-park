> The One Show is where the world's best ideas rise to the top. For over 50 years, the Pencil has stood as one of the most respected honors in advertising, design, and beyond. And at the pinnacle: the Crystal Pencil, awarded only to Best of Discipline winners—peer-to-peer recognition from a jury of over 300 global creative leaders. Winners are celebrated during Creative Week in New York City—a week-long global festival from The One Club for Creativity.
