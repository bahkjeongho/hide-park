> [!Info]
> Andy Iere Kim is a Seoul and LA-based Director of Photography who blends graphic sensibility and evocative lighting into cinematic frames. Informed by diverse cultural experiences, his work transcends boundaries, specializing in visuals that are both trend-driven and deeply resonant. He has collaborated with world-class brands including NIKE, ADIDAS, CHANEL, LVMH, CALVIN KLEIN, VOGUE, APPLE, SAMSUNG, OMEGA, COCA-COLA, SAINT LAURENT, GUCCI, BURBERRY, TOM FORD, and JAEGER-LECOULTRE, while also working with global artists such as Kali Uchis, Kylie Jenner, and A$AP Rocky, alongside leading K-POP entertainment companies. Andy continues to create iconic imagery that resonates and reflects culture worldwide.
