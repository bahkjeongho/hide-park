> Unstated International is a Zurich-based, globally operating design studio run by a passionate team of humans and machines. Since its founding by Leonardo Angelucci and Sabrina Cerea, the practice has been dedicated to merging the physical and digital worlds by bringing together professionals with complementary skills under one roof. Combining a deep interest in emerging technologies with a fascination for the humanities and social studies, the office operates across Art, Design, Fashion, Technology, Culture and Education.
