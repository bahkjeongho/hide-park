> [!Info]
> German Design Award는 건축, 제품, 커뮤니케이션 디자인 분야의 뛰어난 성취를 기리며, 국제적으로 인정받는 혁신적이고 미래 지향적인 상입니다. 이 상은 2012년부터 German Design Council이 수여해 왔으며, 독일의 오랜 디자인 품질 기준인 'Federal Award for Good Design'에 뿌리를 둡니다.
>
> The German Design Award honors outstanding achievements in architecture, product and communication design – internationally recognized, innovative, forward-thinking. The German Design Awards have been presented by the German Design Council since 2012 as an international accolade for outstanding design that makes an impact. Their roots go back to the Federal Award for Good Design, a long-standing benchmark for design quality in Germany.
