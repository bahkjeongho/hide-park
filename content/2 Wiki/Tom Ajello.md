> Tom is a Senior Partner based in Lippincott’s New York office, where he leads the firm’s global Experience, Innovation & Engineering team and helps drive its creative brand and digital work. With more than 20 years at the intersection of creativity and technology, he brings deep expertise in design, digital, and innovation, shaped by work across four continents.
