> The National Film and Television School (NFTS) is a film, television and games school established in 1971 and based at Beaconsfield Studios in Beaconsfield, Buckinghamshire, England. It has been named in The Hollywood Reporter's annual list of the best international film schools for 16 consecutive years, most recently in 2026.
