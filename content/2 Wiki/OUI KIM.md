> [!Info]
> Oui Kim is a Seoul-based director and creative director. With clear direction and a refined visual language, he has developed a style that is bold yet understated. He made his debut with the music video for the band HYUKOH, and quickly gained attention for his work that skillfully combines experimentation with cinematic sensibilities. Since then, he has collaborated with leading K-pop artists such as Aespa and IVE, conveys his vision to a wider audience. Currently working as a creative director, Oui Kim has expanded his practice beyond films to include photography, album direction, and other aspects of visual scenes. Oui Kim continues to refine his own aesthetic that is relatable to others.
