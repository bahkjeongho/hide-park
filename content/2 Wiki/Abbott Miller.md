> [!Info]
> Abbott Miller was born in Indiana and studied design at the [Cooper Union School of Art](http://www.cooper.edu/) in New York. In 1989 he founded the multidisciplinary studio Design/Writing/Research where, in collaboration with Ellen Lupton, he pioneered the concept of “designer as author,” undertaking projects in which content and form are developed in a symbiotic relationship. He joined Pentagram’s New York office as a partner in June 1999.  
  
Abbott’s projects are often concerned with the cultural role of design and the public life of the written word. At Pentagram he leads a team designing books, magazines, catalogs, identities, exhibitions, and creating editorial projects.  
  
Abbott has received numerous design honors, including medals from the [Society for Publication Designers](http://www.spd.org/) and three nominations for [National Magazine Awards](http://www.magazine.org/asme/national-magazine-awards). In 1994 Abbott—together with Ellen Lupton—was awarded the first annual Chrysler Award for Innovation in Design. He is a member of the Type Directors Club, the [Society for Experiential Graphic Design (SEGD)](http://www.segd.org/) and the [Alliance Graphique Internationale (AGI)](http://www.a-g-i.org/). He teaches in the Graphic Design Department of the [Maryland Institute College of Art](http://www.mica.edu/) in Baltimore.  
  
His work and critical writing has appeared in _Eye_, _Print_ and other publications, and he is the co-author of four books, including the classic [_Design/Writing/Research: Writing on Graphic Design_](http://www.amazon.com/Design-Writing-Research-Ellen-Lupton/dp/0714838519/ref=sr_1_1/103-3131563-9477428?ie=UTF8&s=books&qid=1181684141&sr=1-1). A survey of his design work, [_Abbott Miller: Design and Content_](http://amzn.to/1n6Y6hn), was published by Princeton Architectural Press in 2014.
