>`Professional American football team` of [[NFL]] based in [[Cincinnati]]

> [!Info]
> 신시내티에 기반을 둔 미국의 프로 미식축구팀. NFL AFC 북부 디비전에 속하며, 홈구장은 Paycor Stadium이다.

> The Cincinnati Bengals are a professional American football team based in Cincinnati. The Bengals compete in the National Football League (NFL) as a member of the American Football Conference (AFC) North division.
