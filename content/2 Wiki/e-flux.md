> [!Info]
> e-flux was founded by artists in 1999 as an independent platform for contemporary art, culture, and critical thought. Over the past twenty-five years, it has developed into a trusted point of reference for artists, curators, writers, and [institutions](https://e-flux.com/institutions/) worldwide.

At its core, e-flux is a set of publishing and distribution platforms that operate through editorial curation and context rather than algorithmic visibility. Its [announcements](https://e-flux.com/announcements/) services—[e-flux Announcements](https://www.e-flux.com/announcements/e-flux), [Agenda](https://www.e-flux.com/announcements/agenda), [Architecture](https://www.e-flux.com/announcements/architecture), [Education](https://www.e-flux.com/announcements/education), and [Film](https://www.e-flux.com/announcements/video)—deliver news about exhibitions, publications, art fairs, and events to a global audience of art professionals. The [e-flux archive](http://www.e-flux.com/search), comprising more than 60,000 announcements and editorial texts published since 1999, forms a unique record of contemporary artistic and curatorial practice.
