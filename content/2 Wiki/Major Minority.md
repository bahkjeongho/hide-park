> [!Info]
> **Major Minority** is a Seoul-based graphic design studio with a strong focus on typography-driven graphic design. The studio began operating in 2023, founded by designer **Eunji Lee**, who built her professional background in branding design at Blacksheep in London as well as SPC and JOH & Company in Korea, and graphic designer **Ted Hyunhak Yoon**, who previously worked in the Netherlands alongside his practice as a visual artist. Major Minority is currently active across Korea’s cultural and arts sector as well as in the field of branding, presenting a wide range of graphic design projects. Ted Hyunhak Yoon was selected as one of _Monthly Design’s 15 Designers to Watch_ in 2023 and currently teaches graphic design at the Korea National University of Arts and Hongik University.
