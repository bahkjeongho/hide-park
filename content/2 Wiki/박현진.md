> [!Info]
> Hyunjin Park is a visual graphic designer based in Seoul, working on a wide range of work across 2D and 3D. Hyunjin’s original gaze at the world makes the trivial things found in daily life special. Collecting small things in a world and assembling them together, she creates images by adding imagination to share her world. The world expressed by her resembles reality, but different from reality by adding fantasy arouse interest at the point. Rather than simply producing a beautiful image, she want to open the possibility of interpretation by viewers, and try to communicate with people through the visual language drawn on the canvas. Hyunjin is continuing her er work in the hope of giving a special experience in her newly drawn world.
