> [!Info]
> Nicolas Ghesquière는 프랑스-벨기에 패션 디자이너로, 2013년부터 Louis Vuitton의 여성복 컬렉션 아티스틱 디렉터를 맡고 있다.
>
> Nicolas Ghesquière is a French-Belgian fashion designer who has been the Artistic Director of Women’s Collections of the Maison of Louis Vuitton since 2013.
