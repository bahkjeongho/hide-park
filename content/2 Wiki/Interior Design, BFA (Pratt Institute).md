> Become an interior designer in New York City—the global design capital. Study and experiment with industry visionaries committed to the design of the spaces we occupy and develop your craft in our state-of-the-art studios and labs. Consistently ranked one of the best programs in the country for over 20 years, Pratt’s CIDA-accredited Interior Design BFA asks you to redefine how we create and occupy the interior.
