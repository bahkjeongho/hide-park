>`Professional American football team` of [[NFL]] based in [[Atlanta]]

> [!Info]
> 애틀랜타에 기반을 둔 미국의 프로 미식축구팀. NFL NFC 남부 디비전에 속하며, 홈구장은 Mercedes-Benz Stadium이다.
>
> The Atlanta Falcons are a professional American football team based in Atlanta, Georgia, United States. The Falcons compete in the National Football League (NFL) as a member of the National Football Conference (NFC) South division.
