> [!Info]
> We believe that design is, at heart, a social activity that requires collective effort.  
  
Open dialogue and the sharing of ideas is fundamental to our approach. We make time to follow our own investigations, building this into our design process.

The atmosphere in the studio is focused and intense but also friendly and encouraging.  
  
We have a strong team ethic with every project led by founding partners Tony Brook (creative director), Patricia Finegan (managing director) and associate partner Evie Brook (design director). All design work is instigated and generated in-house.  
  
We also have a network of trusted collaborators—programmers, animators and typographers that offer us excellent technical and logistical support.
