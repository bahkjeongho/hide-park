> [!Info]
> Capsule is the Milan-based platform reimagining radical design for today.  Once a year during Milan Design Week, Capsule publishes a hybrid between a magazine and a book, expansive in form and content, delving into the world of design in its broader definition – one that encompasses interiors and architecture, innovation and craft, hospitality and entertainment.  Beyond print, Capsule serves as a 360° creative studio, at the helm of [Capsule Plaza](https://capsule.global/plaza) – a showcase that brings together designers and companies from various creative fields with a bold and multisensory curation – while also producing a clothing and home line.

> [!Summary]
