> The Ontario College of Art and Design University, branded as OCAD University or OCAD U, is a public art university in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. The university is co-educational and operates three academic faculties – the Faculty of Art, the Faculty of Arts and Science, and the Faculty of Design. Established by the Ontario Society of Artists in 1876 as the Ontario School of Art, it is the oldest operating school in Canada dedicated to art and design education.
