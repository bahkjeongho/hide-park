> [!Info]
> A.F.VANDEVORST is a Belgian fashion brand, based in Antwerp.

Designers An Vandevorst and Filip Arickx met in 1987 on their first day of school at the Royal Academy of Fine Arts in Antwerp. An Vandevorst was Dries Van Noten's first assistant for six and a half years.
