>`Master's Program` in playwriting of [[RADA]]

> [!Info]
> 우리는 이야기꾼들이 극작에 과감하고 상상력 있게 접근하고, 예술적 경계를 넓히며, 동시대 극작가이자 연극 창작자, 혁신적 사상가로 성장하도록 힘을 드립니다.
>
> 창작 협업을 중심에 둔 지지적이고 공정한 환경에서, 엄격하고 몰입적인 훈련을 받으며 나만의 목소리를 개발합니다.
>
> We empower storytellers to boldly and imaginatively approach playwriting, push artistic boundaries and thrive as contemporary playwrights, theatre-makers and innovative thinkers.
>
> Working in a supportive and equitable environment with creative collaboration at its heart, you will undertake a rigorous, immersive training while developing your individual voice.
