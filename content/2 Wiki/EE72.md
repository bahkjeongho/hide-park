> EE72 is a global media and entertainment company co-founded by [[Edward Enninful]] and his sister Akua Enninful

Our platform and magazine, _72_, a quarterly print publication spotlights the voices, stories and creative forces shaping culture today.  
   
Thoughtfully curated and fully immersive, EE72 blends the timeless depth of print with the dynamic reach of digital, delivering bold imagery, thought-provoking storytelling and immersive experiences that resonate across borders.  
   
We aim to expand perspectives, inspire connection and invite collaboration.
