>`Bachelor's Program` in Photography and Imaging of [[ArtCenter]]

> [!Info]
> 이미지는 우리의 이야기를 전하고 가장 복잡한 아이디어의 바탕이 됩니다. 사진·이미징 학사(BFA)는 사진가가 비전을 서사로, 영감을 현실로 바꾸도록 갖춰 줍니다. 기술적 역량을 키우고, 사진 개념에 대한 깊은 지식을 쌓으며, 윤리적 책임과 문화적 인식을 갖춘 채 역동적인 환경에서 자신감 있는 전문 사진가가 됩니다. 이 BFA는 긴밀하고 집중된 분위기에서 고급 훈련을 제공합니다.
>
> Imagery tells our stories and grounds our most complex ideas. The Bachelor of Fine Arts in Photography and Imaging degree equips photographers to turn visions into narratives and inspirations into realities. You’ll develop technical prowess, establish deep knowledge of photography concepts, gain ethical responsibility and cultural awareness, and become a professional photographer who is confident in dynamic environments. The BFA in photography and imaging provides high-level training in a close-knit, focused atmosphere. You’ll challenge assumptions about the medium of photography in yourself and in others, fostering new avenues for discovery and expression. You’ll apply traditional and digital photography tools in specific, personal, and broad-reaching investigations of cultural conditions and social innovations. At ArtCenter, we nurture and promote originality, co-create through collaborative projects, and encourage your unique approaches to your craft.
