> [!Info]
> STEFF YOTKA는 에디터, 작가, 크리에이티브 컨설턴트다. i-D의 차기 편집장이다. 이전에는 SSENSE의 콘텐츠 총괄이었고, 《Vogue》와 Vogue Runway에서 패션쇼 리뷰, 글로벌 소셜 보도 총괄, Vogue Runway 앱 개발에 이르는 여러 편집 직책을 맡았다.
>
> 그의 글은 《Vogue》, 《Business of Fashion》, 《Harper’s Bazaar》 등에 실렸다. 강연, 집필 의뢰, 크리에이티브 디렉션, 컨설팅 작업이 가능하다.
>
> STEFF YOTKA IS AN EDITOR, WRITER, & CREATIVE CONSULTANT. she is the incoming editor-in-chief of i-D. previously, she was the head of content at ssense, and held a variety of editorial positions at [vogue](https://vogue.com/) and [vogue runway](https://www.vogue.com/fashion-shows) ranging from reviewing fashion shows to leading global social coverage to developing the Vogue Runway app.
>
> her writing has appeared in [Vogue](https://www.vogue.com/contributor/steff-yotka), [Business of](https://www.businessoffashion.com/reviews/fashion-week/coach-michael-kors-new-york-ready-to-wear-autumn-winter-2023/) [Fashion](https://www.businessoffashion.com/reviews/fashion-week/proenza-schouler-eckhaus-latta-prabal-gurung-new-york-ready-to-wear-autumn-winter-2023/), [Harper’s](https://www.harpersbazaar.com/fashion/fashion-week/a61233726/dries-van-noten-menswear-spring-2025/) [Bazaar](https://www.harpersbazaar.com/fashion/designers/a62490571/the-spiritual-systems-of-sacai/), and more. she is available for speaking engagements, writing commissions, creative direction, and consulting work.
