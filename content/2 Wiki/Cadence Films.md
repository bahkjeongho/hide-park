> [!Info]
> We represent an exclusive roster of directors, and are trusted by the world's most recognizable advertising agencies to deliver their high-end creative.

Our sister company, Cadence Image, and our partners, Management+Artists, represent some of the most influential artists in luxury and premium advertising. Together with them, we also work direct-to-brand with global clients and entertainers, strategizing 360 from idea to final delivery, creating broadcast spots, stills campaigns, social media assets, experiences, and immersive VR. 

Cadence Films has won multiple AICP Awards in Innovation and Entertainment. We have also been awarded several Clios, Ciclope's, and MTV VMA's. Our directors have been nominated for Academy Awards, and have won Best Director and a Palme D’Or at the Cannes Film Festival.
