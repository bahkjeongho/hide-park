> [!Info]
> Dario Amodei(1983년생)는 미국의 인공지능 연구자이자 기업가, 생물물리학자, 계산신경과학자다. 2021년 여동생 Daniela Amodei와 함께 대규모 언어 모델 시리즈 Claude를 만든 회사 Anthropic을 창업했다. 그 전에는 OpenAI의 연구 담당 부사장이었다. 프린스턴 대학교에서 생물물리학 박사 학위를 받았고, 2014~2016년에는 Baidu와 Google Brain에서 머신러닝을 연구했으며, OpenAI에서는 GPT-2, GPT-3, 인간 피드백 기반 강화학습 개발에 참여했다.
>
> Dario Amodei (born 1983) is an American artificial intelligence (AI) researcher, entrepreneur, biophysicist and computational neuroscientist. In 2021, he and his sister Daniela Amodei founded Anthropic, the company behind the large language model series Claude. Before that, he was the vice president of research at OpenAI. At US$965 billion as of May 2026, Anthropic is one of the most valuable AI pure play companies, rivaled by OpenAI.
>
> Amodei earned a doctorate in biophysics from Princeton University. From 2014 to 2016, he worked on machine learning at Baidu and Google Brain. At OpenAI, he worked on the development of GPT-2, GPT-3, and reinforcement learning from human feedback.
